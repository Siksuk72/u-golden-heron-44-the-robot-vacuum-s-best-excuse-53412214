Excuse on make.fun. The dust under the couch filed a restraining order,
so the vacuum respected the boundary and went home to its dock: a
specific spot, a surprising legal twist, and a deadpan retreat to the
one place a robot vacuum calls home. The setup and the payoff are the
same object (the dust under the couch), so the joke lands on the very
spot it was asked about — no apology, just a small machine keeping a
straight face and reporting a legal fact.
